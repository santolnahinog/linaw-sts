        Prepare a report

        A risk reading points to where to look first. It is not proof that anyone did anything wrong. Only investigators and courts can decide that.

        Good reports are specific, factual and easy to act on. Answer four short steps and Linaw writes a clean draft you can copy and send. It is built on your device, and nothing is stored or sent anywhere.

        What did you see?

        Pick the closest match. These are the same categories the DPWH Transparency Portal uses.

        Where is it?

        Location is the most useful detail. Be as exact as you can.

        What exactly looks wrong?

        Describe what you observed, not who you think is responsible.

        Stay safe. Take photos from a public road or space. Never enter a restricted site, confront anyone, or share the draft publicly before you have sent it to an official channel.

        Review and send

        Here is your draft. Copy it, add your contact details if you want a reply, and send it through one of the channels below.

        Where to send it

        These are official channels. Contact details can change, so confirm them on each agency's own website before you send.

        DPWH Transparency Portal

        Find the project on the map, then use its report option and tag it as ghost, unfinished, defective or duplicate. You can attach photos.

        transparency.dpwh.gov.ph
        Contact Center ng Bayan

        Call 16565 or text 0908-881-6565 to file a complaint or report about a government service or official.

        contactcenterngbayan.gov.ph
        Citizens' Complaint Center

        Call or text 8888 for complaints about government services and officials.

        Office of the Ombudsman

        The principal body for investigating graft. It acts on complaints filed in any form.

        ombudsman.gov.ph
