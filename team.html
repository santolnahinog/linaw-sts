How we built it

Five stages, from choosing the issue to checking every fact.

  1. 1

    Pick the issue

    We compared the remaining issues on tech fit, data, interactivity, STS link and safety to present. Corruption scored highest.

  2. 2

    Research

    We studied causes, costs, stakeholders, laws and the 2025 flood control case, using official sources first.

  3. 3

    Plan

    We mapped the pages and the visitor's journey: learn, explore, then act. The site map below shows the result.

  4. 4

    Build

    We wrote the site in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so it runs fast and works without a server.

  5. 5

    Test and verify

    We tested on desktop and phone, and checked every statistic against its source. Corrections are part of the process.
